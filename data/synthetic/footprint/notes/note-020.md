# Decision log - 2025-10-11

**Situation:** A magnitude 5.8 earthquake was reported 32 km from home at depth 10 km, at 03:12. Home is on floor 4 of a building constructed in 1978.

**Risk level I assigned:** critical

**What I did:** Drop-Cover-Hold during shaking, then leave via stairs to open space/assembly point (no elevators), check neighbours, use text not calls.

**Why:** Early action beats perfect information. Shaking first, assessment after.

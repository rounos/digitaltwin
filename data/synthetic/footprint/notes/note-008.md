# Decision log - 2025-05-09

**Situation:** Power outage affecting a city district. Operator estimate: 5.5 h. Outside temperature 20 C. Elderly neighbour on the same floor: yes. Cause: substation fire.

**Risk level I assigned:** high

**What I did:** Prepare for a long outage: fill water containers, charge all devices, move perishables, check on the elderly neighbour; go to parents' place if it passes 6 hours.

**Why:** Several hours is enough to run out of charge and water: prepare before it gets bad.

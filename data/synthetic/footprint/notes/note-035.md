# How I work

- I plan the week on Monday morning.
- Estimates get a 20% buffer.
- Tuesday and Thursday mornings are for deep work: no meetings unless it is the client or it is urgent.
- I prefer written updates over calls.
- If a deadline will slip I say so as early as possible, with options, never on the deadline day.

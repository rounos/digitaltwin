# Decision log - 2025-09-11

**Risk level I assigned:** medium

**What I did:** Stay alert: secure heavy furniture, re-check go-bag, follow official updates (Geodynamic Institute / EMSC). No evacuation.

**Why:** Noticeable but low risk: cheap precautions, no disruption.

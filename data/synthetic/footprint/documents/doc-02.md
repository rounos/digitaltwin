# Personal emergency plan (Eleni)

1. Earthquake: drop-cover-hold, stairs, assembly point at the square. Go-bag by the door.
2. Wildfire: monitor the fire map; leave early if wind is toward home; keep the car above half a tank in summer.
3. Blackout: power bank, torches, fridge closed, check on the elderly neighbour; mum's place as fallback.
4. Heatwave: avoid 11:00-18:00 outdoors, call grandparents.
